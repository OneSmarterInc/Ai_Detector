I reached the bus stop with one shoelace undone and a paper bag slowly tearing at the bottom. Inside were three bread rolls and a jar of jam. The jar was winning.

An older woman on the bench watched me shift the bag from one arm to the other. She opened her shopping trolley, took out a folded cloth bag, and held it toward me without saying anything. I told her I would return it. She asked how I planned to find her again.

That seemed like a fair question. I offered to buy her a coffee instead, but she pointed at the bus turning into the street. We climbed aboard together. I sat near the back while she stayed beside the driver, talking to him about roadworks as if they had been discussing them for years.

Two stops later she got off. I lifted the bag to remind her, and she waved through the window. The bag had a picture of a yellow duck on it. I still use it when I buy bread.
